A letter I hope you keep

For Tahira, My Beautiful Soul

My love,

There are feelings so deep that ordinary sentences feel too small for them. Still, I want to try, because you deserve to know how precious you are to me.

You have become one of the gentlest parts of my world. Your voice can turn a difficult day quiet. Your smile can make a simple moment feel special. Even the smallest things we share have found a permanent little corner inside my heart.

I don't promise a life without misunderstandings. I promise something more real: that when the clouds arrive, I will choose conversation over silence, understanding over pride, forgiveness over keeping score, and us over the argument.

I want to see your dreams become real. I want to celebrate your wins, protect your peace, make you laugh when the day is heavy, and stand beside you when life asks us to be brave.

InshaAllah, if life opens that beautiful door for us, I want our future to be full of dua, family, travel, late-night conversations, a home filled with warmth, and countless ordinary mornings that become precious simply because you are there.

You are my favorite person to tell things to. My safe place. My happiness in the small moments. My beautiful purple tulip in a garden I never knew my heart was waiting for.

And if I ever fail to say it enough: I choose you with a full heart.

Always yours, always cheering for you, always grateful that our paths crossed.

Israr ♡
